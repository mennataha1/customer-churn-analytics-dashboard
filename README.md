# 📊 Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Customer Churn Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze customer behavior, identify churn patterns, and provide actionable business insights to improve customer retention and decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze customer churn behavior.
- Identify the factors affecting customer retention.
- Monitor customer services and payment behavior.
- Track revenue and customer performance.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business users.

## 💡 Business Insights

- Customers with Month-to-Month contracts have the highest churn rate.
- Electronic Check is the most commonly used payment method among churned customers.
- Long-term contracts contribute to lower churn and higher customer retention.
- Customers with higher monthly charges are more likely to churn.
